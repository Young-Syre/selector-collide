# surface of a fallback-based router, which has no Solidity ABI of its own
# because its functions are dispatched by hand inside the fallback.
#
# Modifiers are not part of a selector and solc never records them in an ABI,
# but they are what access control actually is, so a signature list may state
# them: any trailing identifier that is not a Solidity keyword is read as a
# modifier and is used by rule SC002.
